Al Hilal is a professional football club based in the capital Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. It was founded in 1957 and plays its matches in blue and white kits. Fans call the club Al Za'eem, which means The Leader, because it has won more trophies than any other Saudi team. Al Hilal is also one of the most successful clubs in Asian football history. In 2023 the club signed the Brazilian star Neymar, a move that brought huge international attention to the Saudi Pro League and its fast growing global profile.

Al Nassr is a football club from the Saudi capital Riyadh that was founded in 1955. The team plays in yellow and blue, and supporters often call it Al Alami, meaning The Global or The International. Al Nassr became famous around the world in January 2023 when Cristiano Ronaldo joined the club after many years playing in Europe. His arrival made Al Nassr one of the most followed clubs on social media. The team plays its home matches at Al-Awwal Park, a stadium located inside King Saud University in Riyadh.

Al Ittihad is a football club from the coastal city of Jeddah and was founded in 1927 by local players. This makes it the oldest existing club in Saudi Arabia, which is why fans call it Al Ameed, meaning The Dean. The team wears the famous yellow and black stripes, and its supporters are known for their loud and passionate atmosphere. In 2023 Al Ittihad signed the French striker Karim Benzema, a former Ballon d'Or winner. The club plays home games at King Abdullah Sports City, also called The Jewel.

Real Madrid is a Spanish football club from the capital city of Madrid, founded in 1902. The club is known as Los Blancos because of its all white kit. Real Madrid plays at the Santiago Bernabeu stadium, which was named after a former and very influential club president. The club has won the European Cup and Champions League more times than any other team in history. Legendary players such as Alfredo Di Stefano, Zinedine Zidane and Cristiano Ronaldo have all worn the famous white shirt during the club's long history.

FC Barcelona is a football club from the city of Barcelona in the Catalonia region of Spain. It was founded in 1899 by a group led by the Swiss businessman Joan Gamper. The club motto is Mes que un club, which means More than a club, showing its strong link to Catalan identity. Barcelona plays in blue and deep red stripes and is known for its beautiful and attacking passing style. Lionel Messi, who came through the famous La Masia youth academy, became the greatest player in the club's history.

Manchester United is an English football club from the city of Manchester, founded in 1878 under the name Newton Heath. The club is nicknamed The Red Devils and plays at Old Trafford, often called The Theatre of Dreams. In 1958 the club suffered the Munich air disaster, when a plane crash killed eight of its players. Under the Scottish manager Sir Alex Ferguson, who led the team from 1986 to 2013, United won many league titles and trophies and became one of the most popular and valuable football clubs worldwide.

Liverpool is an English football club from the port city of Liverpool, founded in 1892. The team plays in red at Anfield, one of the most atmospheric stadiums in world football. Before every home match, fans sing the famous anthem You'll Never Walk Alone, which has become a symbol of the club. In 2005 Liverpool won the Champions League final in Istanbul after coming back from three goals down at half time against AC Milan. Many fans still remember that night as one of the greatest comebacks in football history.

Bayern Munich is a German football club from Munich in the state of Bavaria, founded in 1900. The club plays in red and white and has dominated the German Bundesliga for decades, winning far more league titles than any rival. Since 2005 Bayern has played its home games at the Allianz Arena, a modern stadium in northern Munich whose outer panels can light up in different colours. Club legends include the elegant defender Franz Beckenbauer and the goal scoring striker Gerd Muller, who were both key players for West Germany.

Juventus is an Italian football club from the northern city of Turin, founded in 1897 by local students. Its nickname is La Vecchia Signora, which means The Old Lady, and the team plays in black and white stripes. For most of its history the club has been linked to the Agnelli family, who also founded the car maker Fiat. Juventus has won more Italian league titles than any other club. Since 2011 the team has played at its own modern stadium, built on the site of the old Delle Alpi.

AC Milan is an Italian football club from Milan, founded in 1899 by English and Italian members, including the Englishman Herbert Kilpin. The team is known as the Rossoneri because of its red and black striped shirts. AC Milan shares the huge San Siro stadium with its city rival Inter Milan, and matches between the two are called the Derby della Madonnina. The club has won the European Cup seven times. The legendary defender Paolo Maldini spent his whole career at Milan and became a true legend of Italian football.
