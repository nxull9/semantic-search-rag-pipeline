A guide to coffee: its origins in Ethiopia and Yemen, the history of coffee houses, the Arabica and Robusta species, how and where coffee plants are grown, the largest producing countries, processing methods, roasting, and brewing methods such as espresso, French press, pour-over and cold brew. It also explains caffeine and its effects on health, and Arabic coffee (qahwa) culture in Saudi Arabia, including cardamom, the finjan cup, hospitality, and Khawlani coffee from the Jazan region.
