An introduction to astronomy and the Solar System: how the Sun and planets formed, the Sun as a star, and facts about each planet, Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune, including their temperatures, atmospheres, moons, rings, storms and orbits. It also covers the asteroid belt, the dwarf planets Ceres and Pluto, the Kuiper Belt and Oort Cloud, comets, and space exploration missions such as the Curiosity and Perseverance rovers, Cassini, New Horizons and Voyager 1.
