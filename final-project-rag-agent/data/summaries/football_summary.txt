Profiles of ten famous football clubs: the Saudi Pro League clubs Al Hilal, Al Nassr and Al Ittihad, and the European clubs Real Madrid, FC Barcelona, Manchester United, Liverpool, Bayern Munich, Juventus and AC Milan. For each club it covers the city, founding year, nicknames, kit colours, home stadium, famous players and coaches, star signings such as Neymar, Cristiano Ronaldo and Karim Benzema, trophies, and historic moments in football such as the Munich air disaster and the Champions League final in Istanbul.
